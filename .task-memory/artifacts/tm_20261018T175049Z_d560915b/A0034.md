V2-043B planning, batch 2 of 2 (the last three ready helpers).

helperMuse-spark-13-xhigh - a dedicated bounded catalog watcher with its own GET /api/event subscription per selected profile, filtering session.created / session.renamed / session.deleted (plus session.forked as the start variant); every trigger calls the existing SessionsController.refresh() for an authoritative re-read and never builds a row locally. It rejects reusing _ProfileSlot/OpenCodeStreamOwner (the slot's close-when-handles-empty semantics, the watched-session hydration gate and the conflation of the live screen's degraded/auth phases with the catalog state). One coalescing timer (about 500-750 ms) plus a dirty flag, at most one pending refresh, and a trigger that arrives while a refresh is in flight joins the in-flight future; no unbounded queues, timers or retry loops. A second concurrent subscription is acceptable under the contract (one global stream, a per-subscriber bounded 4,096 queue, a slow subscriber is disconnected).

helperGemini-38-flash-high - Option A as well: expose Stream<SessionCatalogTrigger> get triggers on SessionCatalogSource and implement it in the IO source with a dedicated lightweight IoSseRecovery. The trigger only signals a change; GET /api/session stays the sole authority and no row is synthesized client-side. The re-read resets the cursor and queries page 1 (limit 50); a trigger during a refresh in flight sets a pending flag and a follow-up runs after it settles; a trigger during a loadMore in flight cancels the paging task, bumps the generation and starts the page-1 refresh. Bounds: a 300 ms debounce, a 1,500 ms ceiling under a flood, a single in-flight gate, and an exponential backoff after transient read failures.

helperClaude-opus-5-xhigh-F (frontier) - Option A in its own sense, but it rejects the dedicated second subscription on a PROJECT RULE the other helpers did not have: v2-plan.md fact 4 states the consequence 'One stream per endpoint; buffer + hydrate on every reconnect; never assume continuity'. Its recommendation is therefore to reuse the single per-profile stream through a catalog lease on the per-profile slot, exposed through a narrow new port (SessionCatalogChanges), with the owner emitting a payload-free catalog signal for the lifecycle frames (including unwatched ones) before the watch filter and the slot counting catalog leases in its refcount. Three findings the other helpers could not see: (1) the live factory is at 1,497 lines against the import_rules limit of 1,500, so a mechanical extraction must come first and the lease logic goes into its own file; (2) the plan already carries a V2-043B line for #365 (the delivered visible catalog), so the new child must take a fresh identifier to avoid overwriting it; (3) the 'behind' notice can reuse the existing l10n.liveStale string, so no locale key and no 14-locale regeneration is needed. Its behaviour: a quiet authoritative sync that walks from the head to the depth already loaded (max(1, _pages), capped at 10 pages and 200 rows), applied all-or-nothing with fresh cursors, without setting loading or stale; a 300 ms debounce plus a 2 s cooldown multiplied by 2^consecutive failures with at most two live timers; a trigger during a sync never cancels it but becomes exactly one final sync; explicit refresh/loadMore cancel a sync (loadMore keeps the pending trigger so the final sync re-reads to the new depth); a failed sync keeps the rows and does not change failure, the state becomes behind; three consecutive failures or a rate limit suspend the auto-sync until a successful manual refresh or a new profile selection; authenticationRequired clears the list and closes the source and the watch; a reconnect increments the epoch and the list is current only when the feed is connected, a read started after the last connection succeeded, no trigger arrived after the read started and there is no suspension. The feed opens after the first successful authoritative read of the profile and only while a SessionsPage is attached (the ShellRoute discards the page on a tab switch; pushing the live or history route does not close it); it closes on a profile or credential change, on an authentication failure, when the last attach detaches and on dispose; a platform without IO or without a directory stays off.
