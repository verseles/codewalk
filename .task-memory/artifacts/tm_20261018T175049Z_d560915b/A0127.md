## V2-044C1 — live timeline viewport (first stage of this child)

**Status of the stage:** implemented and committed locally as the review boundary; review loop in progress. This child (#279) stays open: older-history pagination, jump-to-first and the 2,000-total / 500-resident case belong to the next stage, V2-044C2.

**What the stage delivers** (`lib/features/sessions/session_live_scroll.dart`, `session_live_timeline.dart`, `session_live_page.dart`):
- The live list lands on the newest content and follows it whenever the content grows (a new item or a longer streamed one) while the reader is at the bottom (1 logical px), with a single `jumpTo` per frame, at most three passes.
- A drag, the fling after it, or a wheel stops the follow; the evidence is the public `UserScrollNotification` direction, so the timeline's own jumps never count. The follow resumes at the bottom or through the `live-jump-latest` control, which is marked when content arrived below.
- A settled answer taller than the viewport is revealed from its start once the follow reaches the tail and counts as read; the next turn gives the tail back.
- The controls reuse the retained reference's own strings (`chatGoToLatest`, `chatGoToFirst`) in the scoped 14-language catalog.
