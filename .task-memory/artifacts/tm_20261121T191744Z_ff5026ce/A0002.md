### 1. Summary & Architecture

Deliver `#294` (`V2-055 — Usage, context, errors`) by adding canonical usage capabilities and session-usage hydration/aggregation across `packages/codewalk_core` and `packages/harness_opencode`, and rendering the v1 circular context-usage knob and details popover as a pinnable toolbar action in `WorkspaceAppBarActions`.

- **Evidence (`lib/app/app_preferences_controller.dart:11-280`, `lib/app/workspace_chrome.dart:317-481`):** `WorkspacePinnedAction` currently supports `{servers, settings, newChat}` with a 3-pin cap and default `[WorkspacePinnedAction.newChat]`; `WorkspaceAppBarActions` listens only to `live.turn`.
- **Evidence (`packages/codewalk_core/lib/src/usage.dart:45-169`, `reducer/session_reducer.dart:210-212,960-1002`):** `UsageObservation`, `TokenBreakdown`, `Cost`, `ContextMeter`, `QuotaWindow`, `observeUsage`, and `hydrateUsage` already exist and reconcile cumulative observations by `aggregationIdentity` (`aggregationKey: 'session-total'`) and step observations by `_usageKey` (`id: 'step:<msgId>'`).
- **Evidence (`packages/harness_opencode/lib/src/session_reader.dart:80-173`, `tool/ci/v2_architecture_manifest.json:8`):** `session_reader.dart` is 1,495 lines against the 1,500-line ceiling and `hydrateSession` does not pass `usage:` into `SessionSnapshot`. New projection helpers must live in `session_projection.dart` (162 lines), while moving `_activeSessions`/`_pendingOwners` out of `session_reader.dart` frees ~50 lines.

Architecture flow:
1. `packages/codewalk_core` defines `UsageCapabilities` (`usage.tokens`, `usage.cost`, `usage.context`, `usage.quota`).
2. `packages/harness_opencode` declares `openCodeUsageCapabilities(connectedVersion)` (`tokens`, `cost`, `context` native; `quota` unavailable with reason `'quotaNotExposed'`) and populates `SessionSnapshot.usage` during `hydrateSession` from `GET /api/session/{id}` and hydrated history items (`TimelineItem.meta.message`), plus optional `cost`/`tokens` on `session.compaction.ended`/`failed`.
3. `lib/shared/sessions/session_usage.dart` computes a harness-neutral `SessionUsageView` from `SessionState`, `SessionSelectionState`, and `CapabilitySet` without importing wire types or branching on harness names.
4. `lib/app/app_preferences_controller.dart` adds `WorkspacePinnedAction.contextUsage`, raises the pin cap to 4, defaults fresh installs to `[contextUsage, newChat]`, and runs a one-time migration for existing installs that persisted `cw2.settings.appBarPins`.
5. `lib/app/workspace_chrome.dart` and `lib/features/sessions/session_context_usage.dart` render the circular knob immediately to the left of `New Chat` when an active session is present (`live != null`), hide the toolbar knob when `live == null` while keeping `Context usage` in the 3-dots overflow menu (disabled for opening details when `live == null`, pin toggle always active), and open the details popover with the 2×2 metrics grid, limit/quota status, and "Compact now" action.
