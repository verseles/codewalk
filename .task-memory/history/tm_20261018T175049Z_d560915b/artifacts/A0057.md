Gemini-38: approve, low risk, high confidence, no findings. The unit cleanly implements the session ownership label as a display-only projection of SessionInfo.ownership, adhering to ADR-023 and the plan's five-label vocabulary; the freshness and the list-continuity checks fall back to ownership unknown on stale, expired, future or unproven records, and all 14 locales are covered. Its accepted tradeoffs include that the page relies on the controller's state changes, the refresh and the live events rather than an internal wall-clock ticker to rebuild the rows when a proof exceeds the five-minute display window during idle periods, and that the display window is a fixed constant in the UI projection without dynamic adapter overrides. Its non-issues: the label is brief in every locale, styled labelSmall and inside an unconstrained vertical column, so it wraps without a card overflow; the comparisons are on epoch instants, so a local clock against a UTC observedAt is accurate; and the label grants no mutating capability, which stays with CapabilityContext.denialReason.

The frontier helper (helperClaude-opus-5-xhigh-F): fix_required, low risk, high confidence, in Portuguese. It confirms the projection is pure, follows the §5.3 vocabulary, delegates the freshness to OwnershipInfo.effectiveKindAt and changes neither the OpenCode adapter nor the capability guard; but the freshness is only evaluated when the list rebuilds, so without a controller notification a known label stays visible after the proof ages or expires, violating the decision to show a known kind only with a fresh proof. Its suggested fix: in the controller, after each assignment of _sessions, arm a single Timer for the earliest instant among observedAt plus the display window, expiresAt and a future observedAt of the rows with a known kind, calling _notify(); cancel the timer on clear and dispose; and cover it with an injected-clock test. Its second finding (suggestion, maintainability): the binding current: !controller.stale && !controller.behind has no widget coverage, because the test that names an expired proof actually uses a one-hour-old proof that already shows unknown by age, so the assertion after the failed re-read passes even without the flag, and the stale path (a manual refresh in flight) is never exercised. Its suggested fix: use a fresh proof, assert the known label first, then assert the unknown label after behind and after stale, and either rename the test or add a case with a past expiresAt. Its non-issues: no topology inference, the label never feeds an authority decision, the timezone comparison is on instants, the per-row clock reads differ by microseconds without an observable effect, the switch over OwnershipKind has no wildcard, the packages, the adapter and the transport are unchanged and no new endpoint is read, the architecture guard sees no harness token in the new key and the page stays at 441 lines, the label is a Text without maxLines inside a stretch column, so it wraps at 2x and respects the RTL directionality without moving the Load more control or the headers, and the nonempty source is only guaranteed by the OwnershipProof assert, which fromJson validates.
