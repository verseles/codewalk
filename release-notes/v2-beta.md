> 📣 A cleaner composer: one compact line on phones that opens when you type, with the model chip in your agent's color. On Linux, the window's title bar is now part of the app.

### Composer

- On phones the composer is one compact line until you tap it. The text then moves up and the controls sit in one row below it.
- On desktop it always shows the open layout.
- The model and its variant share one chip, colored by the current agent: green for Build and hot pink for Plan, unless the agent sets its own color. Tap it to change the agent, model or variant.
- The new + and mic buttons point to what is coming: attachments, canned answers and voice. They are disabled for now.

### Desktop window (Linux)

- The title bar is part of the app: drag the empty band to move the window, with minimize, maximize and close on the right.
- Settings > Behavior turns the integrated title bar off and chooses whether closing the window quits or minimizes. Ctrl+W with no tab open follows that choice.
