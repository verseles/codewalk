> 📣 Dictate with your voice: the composer's mic now uses your device's own speech recognition, and its button pulses with your voice.

### Voice input

- Tap the mic to dictate: the words appear in the field as you speak, and a second tap stops.
- While listening, the mic's ball breathes slowly and swells as you speak.
- It uses the system's speech recognition on Android, and on macOS, iOS and browsers that offer it. Where there is none, such as Linux, the mic stays off and says why.
- The first use asks for microphone permission.
