> 📣 Polish: Send becomes an arrow that turns into a check when the agent is done, a softer model chip, flush tabs, and the keyboard steps aside after you send on phones.

### Composer

- Send is now an upward arrow instead of a paper plane.
- When the agent is idle and the field is empty, Send shows a check; it turns back into an arrow when you type or work resumes. The status bar no longer says Idle.
- The model chip shows the agent's color in a softer tint.
- On phones, sending a message closes the keyboard and shrinks the composer.

### Model and agent picker

- The sheet's title is gone, leaving more room for the lists.

### Tabs

- Tabs sit flush on the tab bar, without the one-pixel gap.
- Coming back to the app no longer shows a progress line under the tabs; the composer status and the menu already show the refresh.

### Sessions sidebar

- Tapping the open project collapses or expands its conversations, and its chevron is centered.
