> 📣 CodeWalk 2.9.0 smooths the composer and the return to the app: waiting messages sit at the end of the conversation, the mic takes the idle Send's place on phones, shell commands leave the status bar, and coming back no longer flashes a reconnect.

### Composer and waiting messages

- Shows each message the server holds but has not delivered yet, queued, steering or from another client, as a bubble pinned at the end of the conversation instead of a bar stuck above the composer (`#502`). Its fill is fainter and slowly breathes until delivery (held still with reduced motion), then it becomes an ordinary message in the place history gives it, after everything that arrived while it waited. By design, a waiting message offers no Cancel; sends that failed or are not confirmed keep their Check again line.
- On phones and small windows that can dictate, the mic stands in the idle Send's place and slides back beside Send as soon as there is something to send or work resumes, without ending a dictation (`#501`). Desktop windows keep the idle check, its double-tap Continue and the delivery choice.
- Keeps shell commands out of the composer status bar (`#521`); the timeline and the sub-agents and shells capsule already show them, and the bar reports what came before.

### Returning to the app

- Keeps the event stream when the app comes back after a short trip away, as the official OpenCode client does: a stream that heard from the server, its 15-second heartbeat included, within the last 20 seconds is not reconnected, and a network change still reconnects at once (`#503`).
- For up to 7 seconds after a return, a conversation that was live hides a reconnect that is still settling: no reconnecting label, no hourglass, and the field, attachments, mic, Send and the model picker keep working. A message sent meanwhile is kept and goes out as soon as the conversation is current again, then waits pinned at the end until the conversation shows it; if the refreshed conversation has a staged revert, the draft stays in the field until you tap Send again. A model or agent pick is applied as soon as the connection is back. A server confirmed offline ends the grace at once, and the menu's server alert now waits the same 7 seconds.
